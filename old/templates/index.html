About Us

The PHINMA UI Student Management System aims to make it easier and enhance the management of student information and administrative processes at PHINMA University of Iloilo. The system will automate various aspects of student data handling, such as enrollment, grading, attendance tracking, and communication, to improve operational efficiency and provide a better experience for both students and staff.

Our team consists of dedicated professionals committed to improving educational administration and supporting students and staff. We continuously work on enhancing the system to meet the evolving needs of our users.
